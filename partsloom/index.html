PartsLoom privacy policy

ELSEVANE · Effective for application version 1.3.0 · Prepared October 23, 2026

This document describes the current offline application. A later change introducing payments, online services or data collection requires updated disclosures before release.

What stays on your device

PartsLoom stores your component catalogue, recipes, source/shop identifiers, order and line identifiers, quantities, statuses, saved batch snapshots and checklist marks in the application's private device storage. It also stores your selected interface language. The app does not require buyer names, shipping addresses, payment-card information or marketplace passwords. During CSV import, unmapped columns are not retained in the workspace.

What ELSEVANE receives

The app has no account system, Internet permission, advertising, tracking, analytics or developer-operated data server. It does not transmit your workspace to ELSEVANE. If you voluntarily email support, the information you include is received by the support email provider and used to respond to your request. Please avoid sending customer information or complete order exports.

Files you choose to import or export

Android's system file picker gives access to the specific files or destinations you select. An export or backup is written only when you request it. Your selected provider may be local storage or a third-party cloud service; its own terms and privacy practices apply. Printing uses the Android print service you select. These user-directed actions may send the selected document to that provider or printer. The app does not initiate cloud uploads automatically. Exported JSON and CSV backups are not password-encrypted by the app; choose a trusted destination and protect them as business data.

Retention, changes and deletion

Your workspace remains on the device until you edit or delete it, restore a replacement backup, clear the app's storage or uninstall the app. You can export a backup in Settings. "Delete all local data" removes the local catalogue, orders, batches and checklist state. Backups and exports you saved elsewhere are separate files and must be deleted separately. Android cloud backup is disabled for this app; device security and operating-system behaviour remain controlled by your device.

Payments and children

Version 1.3.0 has no in-app purchases, subscriptions, payment processing or advertisements. PartsLoom is a professional preparation tool for makers and is not designed or marketed for children. It does not request age, contact-list, camera, microphone or location access.

Contact

For support, privacy questions and requests, contact support.elsevane@gmail.com. Identify PartsLoom and your app version. ELSEVANE cannot remotely retrieve or delete a workspace that has never been transmitted to it.

This policy is a factual description of the current build. It does not represent a guarantee of compliance in every jurisdiction or replace the owner's required store, merchant and legal-entity disclosures.
