PartsLoom privacy policy

ELSEVANE · Describes application versions 1.3.0–1.5.1 · Revised November 6, 2026

This document describes the current offline application, including the optional one-time PartsLoom Pro purchase through Google Play. A later change introducing online services or data collection requires updated disclosures before release.

What stays on your device

PartsLoom stores your component catalogue, recipes, source/shop identifiers, order and line identifiers, quantities, statuses, saved batch snapshots and checklist marks in the application's private device storage. It also stores your selected interface language. The app does not require buyer names, shipping addresses, payment-card information or marketplace passwords. During CSV import, unmapped columns are not retained in the workspace.

What ELSEVANE receives

The app has no account system, Internet permission, advertising, tracking, analytics or developer-operated data server. It does not transmit your workspace to ELSEVANE. If you voluntarily email support, the information you include is received by the support email provider and used to respond to your request. Please avoid sending customer information or complete order exports.

Files you choose to import or export

Android's system file picker gives access to the specific files or destinations you select. An export or backup is written only when you request it. Your selected provider may be local storage or a third-party cloud service; its own terms and privacy practices apply. Printing uses the Android print service you select. These user-directed actions may send the selected document to that provider or printer. The app does not initiate cloud uploads automatically. Exported JSON and CSV backups are not password-encrypted by the app; choose a trusted destination and protect them as business data.

Retention, changes and deletion

Your workspace remains on the device until you edit or delete it, restore a replacement backup, clear the app's storage or uninstall the app. You can export a backup in Settings. "Delete all local data" removes the local catalogue, orders, batches and checklist state. Backups and exports you saved elsewhere are separate files and must be deleted separately. Android cloud backup is disabled for this app; device security and operating-system behaviour remain controlled by your device.

Payments and children

PartsLoom offers one optional one-time in-app purchase, PartsLoom Pro, processed by Google Play under Google's terms and privacy policy. ELSEVANE does not receive your payment details; the app receives only the purchase status (product, purchase state, Google purchase token and signature) to unlock Pro on your device. There are no subscriptions or advertisements. PartsLoom is a professional preparation tool for makers and is not designed or marketed for children. It does not request age, contact-list, camera, microphone or location access.

Contact

For support, privacy questions and requests, contact support.elsevane@gmail.com. Identify PartsLoom and your app version. ELSEVANE cannot remotely retrieve or delete a workspace that has never been transmitted to it.

Email support and requests

If you contact support, ELSEVANE receives your email address, any sender name, your message, mail metadata and any attachments you choose to send. The mailbox uses Gmail; Google processes correspondence under its privacy policy. Correspondence is used for your request and related follow-up. Start with the version and reproduction steps, preferably with invented examples; do not send passwords or complete customer or seller reports by default.

Retention depends on the support purpose, necessary legal obligations or an unresolved dispute. There is no configured automatic expiry; removal is manual. You may request access, correction or deletion at the contact above, subject to applicable requirements and necessary legal retention. Clearing the app does not delete correspondence or provider copies. App-local data cannot be retrieved remotely by ELSEVANE.

Policy website and changes

The public copy of this policy is hosted on GitHub Pages. Visiting it exposes connection/request information to GitHub under its privacy statement. This policy page contains no added analytics, forms, embedded third-party media or tracking scripts. The offline Android app does not perform cross-site tracking or use browser Do Not Track signals. Other providers may have their own practices.

Material changes to app or support data handling require a revised policy and any required notices or consent before the changed handling begins. The revision date above identifies this document. Online accounts, analytics or external processing are not introduced by this policy revision; the only purchase is the optional Google Play one-time PartsLoom Pro unlock described above.

This policy is a factual description of the current build. It does not represent a guarantee of compliance in every jurisdiction or replace the owner's required store, merchant and legal-entity disclosures.
