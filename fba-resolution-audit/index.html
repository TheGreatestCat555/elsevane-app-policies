Privacy policy — your files and data

Updated 26 October 2026. Covers Android closed-alpha builds 0.13.7–0.13.8 and the companion browser workbench.

FBA Resolution Audit is an ELSEVANE tool for reviewing shipment and reimbursement records. This policy describes the current Android app and its companion browser workbench. Contact: support.elsevane@gmail.com.

Files you choose and information you enter

The app reads reports and saved audits that you choose, and the account labels, report context, mappings, notes and review decisions you enter. Reports can contain shipment and item identifiers, dates, quantities, financial amounts and any other information present in their original columns. Imported audit JSON can contain the complete original file bytes, including unmapped columns.

This information is used to validate your inputs, recalculate the audit, explain evidence and prepare the exports you request. The app does not sign into Amazon, request Amazon credentials or submit claims. Importing a report does not change the original source file.

Local processing

The current app processes audit data on your device. It has no app server for reports, analytics, advertising or automatic report uploads. It has no account login or active purchases. The embedded app view is restricted to packaged local content with network loads blocked.

Google Play adds an installer check to the version it distributes, including the com.android.vending.CHECK_LICENSE permission. This check uses the Google Play Store to check whether the app was installed through Google Play and can direct an unofficial copy to its Play listing. The Play Store may need internet to refresh this verification; audit processing remains local. Google handles its store and security services under its own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy . ELSEVANE does not operate a server that receives your audit files.

These app restrictions do not control Android, your email application or a document provider you choose. Such software may have its own connections and data practices. Debug test APKs enable WebView debugging; they are development builds, not production security releases.

Android working data

The Android app saves its working audit, an unsaved previous audit when retained, note and review drafts, and reusable import profiles in app-private storage. The working file uses authenticated encryption with a key held in Android Keystore. The app excludes its private working data from Android backup.

Clearing the current session can retain it as the previous audit; it is not the same as deleting all local audit data. Private working data remains until you remove it, uninstall or clear app storage, or it is lost through device or system failure. Encryption is not a substitute for an independent backup.

Before an Android export opens a destination picker, the app keeps a random reference for that attempt in private storage excluded from Android backup. This reminder contains no report text, filename, destination address or stable device identifier. It is removed after the interface acknowledges the result, after cancellation without a selected destination, or when you deliberately dismiss the reminder. An interrupted export can leave a complete or partial external file; the reminder does not confirm an audit backup.

Exports and document providers

You choose where to save exports. Audit JSON contains original source information, saved notes and review decisions; it does not include unsaved drafts or the separate import-profile library. Profiles have their own JSON backup. CSV and readable HTML contain audit findings and source references, and HTML includes saved notes.

These JSON, CSV and HTML exports are readable files; the app does not encrypt them. Anyone who can access the selected destination may be able to read them. The app asks the Android picker for local documents, but this request does not guarantee that a provider will keep files offline or prevent its own synchronization. The provider's access, retention and synchronization settings apply.

The app does not delete your source documents or external exports when you clear app data. Manage those files and any synchronized copies at their destinations.

Companion browser workbench

The browser workbench keeps the active audit, drafts and profiles in memory. Closing or reloading the page can lose this in-memory work. You can explicitly save a recovery copy of committed audit data in the browser's IndexedDB storage; that copy is unencrypted and excludes drafts, the previous audit and profiles.

There is one recovery slot for the site in a browser profile. Another tab can replace it, and browser cleanup can remove it. Recovery is not a confirmed external backup. Browser exports are also readable files.

Deleting local data and recovering work

Help → Privacy and your files → Delete all local audit data removes the app's current and retained previous audit, drafts and profiles. On Android it removes the private working file and its encryption key. In the browser it deletes the recovery slot and clears this page's working session.

This does not erase source files, downloaded or exported files, copies held by other browser tabs, provider backups or email. Handle those copies separately. Device loss, uninstall or unavailable encryption keys can make private working data unrecoverable. ELSEVANE has no server copy of your audit to restore; keep independent audit JSON and profile backups where appropriate.

Full local reset also attempts to remove the export reminder. If the audit is deleted but the reminder cannot be removed, the interface clears the audit and keeps a separate warning. Source documents and external destinations are not deleted by that warning or by dismissing it.

Contacting support

You can email support.elsevane@gmail.com yourself. The app sends no reports or messages and adds no audit attachments. Your email provider and the support mailbox receive the address, sender name, message, mail metadata and attachments that you choose to send. Start with the app version and steps to reproduce the issue; do not send complete seller reports or credentials by default.

ELSEVANE uses ordinary Gmail for this support address. Correspondence is used for the request and related follow-up, not advertising or sale. Retention depends on that purpose and any necessary legal obligations or unresolved dispute. There is no automatic expiry: deletion and access, correction or deletion requests sent to the same address are handled manually. Clearing app data does not erase email. Google applies its own email-service privacy practices. No support bot or external AI processing is active for this support channel.

About this closed alpha

This experimental build has no active purchases, advertising or cloud account. Built-in sample records are synthetic; real seller-export compatibility and reimbursement eligibility have not been validated. Later changes to data handling require a policy update and a review of the actual build and store declarations. No Google Play approval is implied.
